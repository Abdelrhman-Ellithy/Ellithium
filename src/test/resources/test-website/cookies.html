Cookies & Web Storage

Full coverage of CookieActions: add, get by name, get all, delete named, delete object, delete all. Includes Secure/SameSite/HttpOnly attributes and localStorage/sessionStorage management via JS executor.

CookieActions.addCookie CookieActions.getCookieNamed CookieActions.getCookies CookieActions.deleteCookieNamed CookieActions.deleteCookie CookieActions.deleteAllCookies JavaScriptActions (localStorage/sessionStorage)
➕ Add Cookie (CookieActions.addCookie)
Creates a new cookie via driver.manage().addCookie(). Fill name and value, then inspect the result with getCookieNamed.
🔍 Get Cookies (getCookieNamed / getCookies)
Read a specific cookie by name, or retrieve all cookies as a Set. Mirrors CookieActions.getCookieNamed(name) and getCookies().
🗑 Delete Cookies (deleteCookieNamed / deleteAllCookies)
Tests CookieActions.deleteCookieNamed(name) and CookieActions.deleteAllCookies(). After deletion, getCookieNamed returns null.
